# FitMeal – Executive Business Dashboard

## Dashboard Overview
The FitMeal Executive Master Dashboard is an interactive business analytics dashboard designed to monitor and evaluate the overall business performance of FitMeal, a healthy meal subscription startup.

It provides management with a visual overview of important business metrics, customer behaviour, financial performance, marketing effectiveness, and growth opportunities to support data-driven decision-making.

## Key Analysis Areas
- **Business Performance:** Overall business performance and key performance indicators (KPIs).
- **Financial Analysis:** Revenue, costs, profitability, and financial performance.
- **Customer Analytics:** Customer behaviour, acquisition, retention, and subscription trends.
- **Marketing Analytics:** Marketing channel performance and customer acquisition effectiveness.
- **Customer Feedback:** Customer satisfaction and feedback analysis.
- **Plan vs Actual Analysis:** Comparison of pilot performance against planned targets.
- **Market & Competitor Analysis:** Market opportunities and competitor positioning.

## Dashboard Objectives
- Monitor important business KPIs.
- Identify business strengths and improvement areas.
- Understand customer and subscription trends.
- Evaluate marketing channel effectiveness.
- Support strategic planning and management decisions.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel – Data preparation and analysis.
- Google Sites – Dashboard publishing and presentation.
- Business Analytics – KPI monitoring and performance evaluation.

## Project Purpose
This dashboard demonstrates how business data can be transformed into meaningful visual insights to support management decisions, improve operational performance, and identify future growth opportunities.

## Data Disclaimer
The project uses synthetic/illustrative data for educational and portfolio purposes. It does not represent actual FitMeal company operations.
