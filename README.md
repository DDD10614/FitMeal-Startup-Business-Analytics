# FitMeal Startup – Business Analytics & Executive Dashboard

## Project Overview

FitMeal is a healthy meal subscription startup business analytics project focused on evaluating customer behavior, financial performance, marketing effectiveness, subscription performance, and business growth opportunities.

This project combines Excel-based business analysis, financial planning, customer insights, and an interactive executive dashboard to support data-driven business decisions.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze customer acquisition and retention.
- Evaluate revenue, costs, and profitability.
- Understand customer feedback and satisfaction.
- Measure marketing channel performance.
- Analyze subscription plans and customer preferences.
- Evaluate business performance against financial projections.
- Identify growth opportunities and operational improvements.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel – Data analysis, formulas, financial modelling and reporting.
- Business Analytics – KPI analysis and performance evaluation.
- Google Sites – Interactive dashboard publishing.
- Microsoft Word – Business analysis documentation.
