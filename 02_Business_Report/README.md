# CoinBridge P2P – Business Research Report

## About This Folder
This folder contains the comprehensive business research report for CoinBridge P2P, an AI-enabled peer-to-peer cryptocurrency trading platform focused on fraud detection, identity verification, payment security, and regulatory compliance.

## Report Contents

1. Business Domain Overview
2. Industry Challenges
3. Major Business Problem – Fiat Payment Fraud
4. Proposed AI-Enabled Solution Architecture
5. SWOT Analysis
6. Core Business and Regulatory Insights
7. Strategic Business Recommendations
8. 12-Month Implementation Roadmap
9. Conclusion and References

## Key Areas Covered
- AI-powered KYC and identity verification
- Deepfake and fake document detection
- Bank account verification using penny-drop
- AI-based transaction monitoring and risk scoring
- Blockchain wallet screening
- AML, CFT, PMLA and FIU-IND compliance considerations
- USDT P2P payment security and fraud prevention

## Project Objective
To develop a strategic business and technology roadmap for reducing fraudulent P2P transactions, strengthening customer verification, improving platform security, and supporting regulatory compliance in India.
